How Customers Find Us

The questions readers arrive with, and where to find the answers.

Plenty of people read before they call. They want to know whether the noise, the drip or the warm air is something they can sort out themselves. These are the questions that bring readers to the blog.

"What is wrong with it?"

The most frequent searches are simply “ac problem”, “ac why not cooling” and “reasons for air conditioner not cooling”. Others describe what they see or hear: “ac noise”, “air conditioner sounds loud”, “vibration sound in ac”, “ice in air conditioner” and “why water from ac” dripping. A smaller group notices behaviour, such as “ac automatically turns off after a few minutes” or “ac fan speed low or auto”.

"Can I fix it myself?"

We see “how to fix ac”, “ac troubleshooting guide” and “hvac troubleshooting guide” all the time. Our short answer is yes for filters, remotes and breakers, and no for gas and electrics. For routine care, “ac maintenance at home” and “window ac service at home” are the ones where we try to say plainly what is safe to do and what isn't.

Background questions

Some readers just want to understand what they own. They search “how many types of gas used in ac”, “room calculation for ac” or “ac work” to learn how an air conditioner does what it does. Others go straight to “air conditioners repair” or “aircon tech near me” when reading has convinced them the job needs a professional.

Reading before calling

Some readers are just curious how the whole “hvac system” works, or search “air conditioning maintenance” in general, before deciding whether to call anyone. Others ask about “air conditioner kinds”, or how “duct works” fit into a central system.

FAQ — الأسئلة الشائعة

Common Questions Before You Read

Quick answers, with links to the full guides.

Which guide should I read first if my AC stopped cooling?+
Start with our AC not cooling guide. It walks through the seven most common causes in order of how easy they're to check, so you can rule out the simple ones before paying for a visit.
Can I fix an AC problem myself?+
Some checks are safe: cleaning the filter, replacing remote batteries, checking the breaker and looking for a blocked drain. Gas, capacitors, control boards and anything involving live wiring should be left to a technician, because mistakes there are costly and dangerous.
Where can I look up an AC error code?+
Our AC error codes guide has tables for Gree, LG, Daikin, Midea and more. Codes vary by model, so treat the table as a starting point and call us if the code returns after a reset.
Why is my electricity bill so high in summer?+
The usual culprits are a clogged filter or coil, low refrigerant, an old unit and a thermostat set far below what the room needs. A clean, correctly charged AC runs shorter cycles. Our cleaning guide explains how often to service it.
What temperature should I set my AC to in Riyadh?+
Most technicians recommend 24 to 26 degrees. Going lower rarely cools the room faster; it just keeps the compressor running longer and raises the bill.
Are the prices in your guides current?+
They're 2026 estimates based on our own jobs in Riyadh. The final price always depends on the unit and the fault, and we confirm it in writing after inspection. The cost guide lists what each job includes.
Do you have guides for washing machines and fridges too?+
Yes: washing machine repair and fridge repair, each with common faults, costs and repair-or-replace advice.
Is an AC maintenance contract worth it?+
It usually pays off if you have several units, rent out property, or run a business where a breakdown costs money. For one unit in a flat, a twice-yearly cleaning is often enough.
