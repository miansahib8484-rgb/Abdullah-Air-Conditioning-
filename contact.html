Booking and Contact Questions

Everything you need to know before you call or message us.

What is the fastest way to reach an AC technician in Riyadh?+
WhatsApp. Send your district, a photo of the unit and a line about the fault to WhatsApp or call +966 55 292 5939. We usually reply within minutes and confirm a technician and arrival time.
What are your working hours?+
Sunday to Thursday from 7 AM to 10 PM, and Friday from 2 PM to 10 PM. Emergency AC, washer and fridge calls are answered 24 hours a day, including holidays, at the same price.
What should I tell you when I call?+
Your district, the brand and type of unit, what it's doing (not cooling, leaking, noisy, showing a code) and any error code on the display. That lets the technician bring the right parts and often saves a second trip.
Can I book a specific time slot?+
Yes. We offer same-day slots when the schedule allows and can book ahead for cleaning, installation and contract visits. We confirm the slot by message so you have it in writing.
Do you charge if I decide not to go ahead with the repair?+
A diagnostic visit costs 50 to 100 SAR. If you go ahead with the repair, that fee is deducted from the final invoice.
Which payment methods do you accept?+
Cash, credit cards and mada. You pay after the work is finished and you have checked it, and you receive an official tax invoice.
Which parts of Riyadh do you cover?+
Every district, including Al Malqa, Al Yasmin, Hittin, Al Nuzha, Al Olaya, Al Quds, Al Malaz, Al Rawdah, King Fahd, Al Aqiq, Al Ghadir and Ash Shifa. We don't operate outside Riyadh.
Can I visit your office?+
Yes, we're in Al-Quds on Prince Mohammed bin Faisal bin Turki Road. Call ahead because our technicians are usually out on jobs. Repairs are done at your home or workplace, not at our office.

How Customers Find Us

The exact words people type when they need someone today.

By the time someone lands on this page they're usually one tap from calling, and what they typed to get here tells us a lot. These are the patterns we see.

Asking for a person

The biggest group searches “ac technician near me”, “ac technician riyadh” or just “ac technician”. A few use the wording “near me ac technician”, and others say “aircon technician near me”. Those who think of the trade rather than the title search “ac mechanic near me” or “ac mechanic”. In every case we need the same three things: your district, the brand of the unit and what it's doing.

Asking for a repair service

Another large group asks for the service: “ac repair service near me”, “ac repair service riyadh” and “ac repair riyadh near me”, or the aircon spelling “aircon repair service near me”. Some write it in full as “air conditioner repair service near me”. If you can send a photo of the fault, we can usually give a price range before the technician leaves.

Asking for servicing

Not every visit is a repair. People also search “ac service near me”, “ac service near by me”, “ac maintenance near me”, “air conditioning service near me” and “aircon service near me”. Reversed word order such as “service ac near me” and “service air conditioner near me” shows up constantly on mobile. And the shortest of all is “ac near me”, which we read as "somebody, please".

Booking-specific searches

A few searches are really about booking rather than the fault itself: people want their “ac fixed” today, or search “ac repair replacement near me” when they aren't yet sure whether it needs a repair or a new unit. Others search “central air repair near me” or “best air conditioner repair near me” and expect a call back within minutes.
