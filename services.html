Questions About Our Services & Pricing

Everything you need to know before booking
How much does AC repair cost in Riyadh?+
AC repair prices start from 150 SAR for simple faults and rise depending on the fault type and spare parts needed. Compressor replacement ranges from 500 to 1,500 SAR depending on size and brand. We give you the full price before starting work — no surprises.
How much does new split AC installation cost?+
Split AC installation starts from 350 SAR and includes: installing both indoor and outdoor units, copper pipe connection, electrical wiring, and a full test run. Cost varies depending on the distance between units and connection difficulty.
Do you refill freon (AC gas)?+
Yes, we refill all refrigerant types: R22, R32, and R410A. We first check for and fix any leak before recharging, to prevent the problem recurring. Gas refill pricing ranges from 150 to 300 SAR depending on gas type and amount.
How often does an AC need cleaning in Riyadh?+
In Riyadh's dry, dusty climate, our experts recommend cleaning the AC at least once every 6 months — before and after summer. Regular cleaning cuts the electricity bill by 15-25% and extends the AC's lifespan.
Do you repair front-load and top-load washing machines?+
Yes, we repair both types. Front-load and top-load washing machines from all brands: Samsung, LG, Haier, Toshiba, White Westinghouse, Kiriazi, and more. Common faults: motor, drum bearing, drain pump, PCB board, door lock.
Do you use genuine or aftermarket spare parts?+
We only use genuine manufacturer parts — never aftermarket or generic substitutes. We carry the most common parts for major brands in our vans, ensuring same-day repair in most cases. Warranty only applies to genuine parts.
