Questions About Our AC Services & Prices

What each service includes, what it costs and how booking works.

What AC services do you offer in Riyadh?+
Repair, chemical cleaning, gas refill, installation and relocation, annual maintenance contracts and commercial or central HVAC work. We also repair washing machines and refrigerators, so one company can look after most of the cooling and laundry equipment in your home.
How much does split AC installation cost?+
From 350 SAR. That covers mounting the indoor and outdoor units, connecting the copper pipe, wiring and a full test run. Long pipe runs or difficult outdoor positions cost more, and we say so before starting. Window AC installation starts from 150 SAR. Installations carry a 1-year written warranty.
What does an AC cleaning include, and when should I book it?+
A full chemical wash of the indoor coil and filters, drain-line flush, outdoor unit wash and an anti-bacterial treatment, from 120 SAR per unit. In Riyadh most homes need it every six months, before and after summer.
How much is an AC gas refill?+
R32 from 150 SAR, R410A from 180 SAR and older R22 from 300 SAR. Gas is never simply used up, so if a unit is low we find the leak first. Leak repair typically runs 200 to 600 SAR depending on where the leak is.
Does my AC need a gas refill or just cleaning?+
If cooling is weak and you see ice on the pipes or hear hissing, it's likely gas. If airflow is weak, the unit smells or water drips indoors, cleaning usually fixes it. A technician can tell in minutes with gauges, so it's worth checking before paying for either.
How does an annual AC maintenance contract work?+
You get two to four scheduled visits a year for inspection and filter cleaning, priority booking for emergencies and a discount on any repair that turns up. Contracts start from 800 SAR per year and get cheaper per unit when you cover several units.
Do you handle central AC and commercial HVAC?+
Yes. We service offices, shops, hotels and warehouses, including VRF and VRV systems, chillers, air handlers, fan coil units and ductwork. A site visit is free and you receive a detailed written quote within 24 hours.
Can one technician fix my AC and my washing machine in the same visit?+
Often, yes. Tell us about both faults when you book, and we send someone who carries parts for each. It saves you a second appointment and a second call-out.
Do you use genuine spare parts?+
Yes, genuine manufacturer parts only. Warranty applies to genuine parts, so we don't fit generic substitutes. On very old units a part may be discontinued; we tell you that at the visit instead of improvising a fix that won't last.
How do I get a price before you visit?+
Send a photo or short video of the unit and the fault on WhatsApp or call +966 55 292 5939. We give a first estimate on the spot and confirm the final price in writing after the technician inspects the unit.
هل تقدمون خدمة فك وتركيب ونقل المكيفات؟+
نعم، نفك المكيف ونعيد تركيبه في موقعه الجديد داخل الرياض، مع فحص الأنابيب النحاسية والتمديدات الكهربائية وتجربة التشغيل قبل الانتهاء. تبدأ التركيبات من 350 ريال، والضمان سنة مكتوبة.
هل تصلحون المكيفات المركزية والتجارية؟+
نعم، نصلح ونصون المكيفات المركزية وأنظمة التكييف التجارية للمكاتب والمحلات والفنادق والمستودعات. المعاينة مجانية ونرسل عرض السعر المفصل خلال 24 ساعة.
هل يوجد عقد صيانة سنوي للمكيفات؟+

How Customers Find Us

How people describe the services they need when they search.

Nobody searches for servicing in a panic. It's the landlord before summer, the facility manager with a yearly budget, the family that has just moved in and has a bare wall where an AC should be. Here's how they usually put it.

Routine servicing

Most people start with “ac service” or “ac maintenance”. Some use the shorter “aircon service” and “aircon maintenance”, and others write it as “air conditioner maintenance”. They all end up in the same place: filters, coils, drain and gas pressure checked before the heat arrives.

With "near me" attached

On a phone, the location is part of the query. We see “ac servicing near me”, “air conditioner servicing near me” and “home ac service near me”, and when someone suspects a fault as well, “ac service and repair near me” or “air conditioning service and repair near me”. Our answer doesn't change with the wording: give us your district and we book the slot.

Unit, system, or the whole building

People who own several units tend to search for the equipment rather than the job: “ac unit maintenance”, “air conditioning unit maintenance”, “ac system maintenance” and “air conditioning system maintenance”. If that's you, an annual contract with two to four visits a year usually works out cheaper than one-off calls.

New units and moves

Tenants and new homeowners search “ac installation near me”, and apartment owners with older blocks often need “window ac installation near me”. We mount, pipe, wire and test the unit, and give a written warranty on the installation.

Commercial and central systems

Business customers search differently. We see “hvac services”, “hvac maintenance” and “hvac repair”, along with “hvac technician”, “hvac contractor” and “hvac companies in riyadh”. Some write “air conditioning contractors” or “commercial hvac near me”, and others the exact problem: “central ac repair” or “ductwork air conditioning”. Offices, shops, hotels and warehouses get a free site visit and a written quote within 24 hours.

When the appliance list is longer than the AC

Owners who bundle jobs use searches like “home appliance service and repair”, “home service appliance repair”, “appliance repair & service” and “same day appliance repair”. We repair washing machines and refrigerators alongside air conditioners, so one call can cover all of it.

Commercial and specialised searches

Facility managers and larger accounts search differently again: “hvac solutions” and “ac units maintenance” for multi-unit contracts, “commercial hvac companies near me” for a company with several branches, and “split ac service near me” for a single flat that just needs a routine visit. Some just want a seasonal “ac tune up” before the heat arrives.
