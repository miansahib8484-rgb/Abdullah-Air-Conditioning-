Personal Data Protection Policy

1. Who We Are

Abdullah Air Conditioning, a registered Saudi company based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. We provide AC and home appliance repair, installation, and cleaning services. Contact us: +966 55 292 5939

2. Data We Collect

When you contact us via the website, WhatsApp, or phone, we may collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Service address (district and area)
  • Details about the appliance and fault to be repaired
  • Date and time of site visits (via server data)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Coordinate your service appointment and technician visit
  • Issue the tax invoice
  • Follow up after service to ensure your satisfaction
  • Send our AC tips newsletter (if you subscribe voluntarily)

We never sell your data to any third party. We do not use it for external marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing

Your data may be shared with: our own technicians (to coordinate the visit only), WhatsApp to complete communication, and the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) when issuing tax invoices in compliance with Saudi regulations.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide the service, per Saudi regulatory requirements (5 years for tax records). We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights

Under the Saudi PDPL, you have the right to: access your data, correct it, request its deletion (within legal limits), and object to its processing. To contact us regarding your data: +966 55 292 5939

7. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Our hosting server keeps standard access logs for technical and security purposes only.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be published on this page along with an updated last-review date. Last updated: June 2025.
