📋 What Each Price Actually Includes

1

Diagnostic Visit

50–100 SAR

The fee for the technician to come, inspect and diagnose. With most serious companies it's deducted in full from the repair invoice if you go ahead. Be wary of anyone who charges a high inspection fee (150 SAR or more) without a clear reason.

2

Gas Refill: R32, R410A and R22

150–300 SAR

The price depends on the refrigerant: R32 (the newest) from 150 SAR, R410A from 180 SAR and old R22 from 300 SAR because it's now hard to source. If there's a real leak rather than a slow natural loss, the leak repair is added, usually between 200 and 600 SAR depending on where it's. Not sure it's gas? Read the not-cooling guide.

3

General Repair: Filter, Drain, Sensor

From 150 SAR

Covers cleaning or replacing a filter, clearing a blocked drain, or replacing a faulty temperature sensor. These are among the cheapest and fastest faults, usually solved in 30 to 60 minutes in the same visit.

4

Control Board (PCB) Repair

350–600 SAR

Common on modern inverter units. Symptoms include the AC ignoring the remote, blinking an unclear code or switching itself off suddenly. The price varies with the availability of the genuine part for the brand, and Gree, LG and Daikin boards don't cost the same. Check the error-code guide first.

5

Compressor Repair or Replacement

From 900 SAR

Always the most expensive because it's the heart of the AC. A repair starts around 900 SAR, while a full replacement on a 1.5-ton unit can pass 1,800 SAR. The practical rule: if the repair costs more than 50 to 60 percent of a new unit of the same capacity, or the AC is over eight years old, replacing is often cheaper in the long run. If your AC runs but doesn't cool, read that first, because you may not need a compressor at all.

6

Deep Chemical Cleaning

From 120 SAR

Includes washing the indoor coil, the filter and the outdoor unit. There's usually a discount when several units are cleaned in one visit, for example a home with four or five ACs. In Riyadh dust we recommend cleaning every four to six months to avoid costlier faults later. Full cleaning guide here.

7

New AC Installation

Quoted after inspection

Depends on capacity (1.5 ton, 2 ton, central), the length of copper piping and the position of the outdoor unit. Central and commercial systems always need an on-site inspection before any price, and we don't give rough phone figures for these because they're usually wrong. Simple split installation starts from 350 SAR.

🔍 4 Factors That Drive AC Unit Repair Pricing

🔢 AC capacity

A 2-ton unit needs more gas and larger parts than a 1-ton unit, so the price rises with size.

🏷️ Brand

Parts for Daikin and Mitsubishi are often dearer than Gree or Midea because of availability.

📅 Age of the unit

Parts for AC units of 10 years or more can be scarce or discontinued, which raises the cost.

🛡️ Warranty offered

A company that gives a written 90-day warranty may charge slightly more than a technician with none. The difference is worth it.

⚠️ Red Flags: Signs You Are Being Overcharged

  • A final price over the phone with no inspection or photo
  • No official tax invoice
  • Undeclared "emergency" surcharges
  • No written warranty on the part or the labour
  • Refusal to show you the old part they replaced
  • Asking for full payment before the work has started

FAQ About AC Repair Cost in Riyadh

How much does an AC diagnostic visit cost in Riyadh?+
Between 50 and 100 SAR. If you go ahead with the repair, that fee is deducted from the final invoice, so the diagnosis is effectively free when we do the work.
How much does an AC gas refill cost in Riyadh?+
R32 starts at 150 SAR, R410A at 180 SAR and R22 at 300 SAR. If the system has a real leak rather than a slow natural loss, add 200 to 600 SAR for the leak repair.
What is included in the "from 150 SAR" repair price?+
The visit, the diagnosis and minor fixes such as a filter, a blocked drain or a temperature sensor. Parts for bigger jobs, like a capacitor, fan motor or control board, are quoted separately before we start.
Why do AC repair prices differ so much between companies?+
Genuine or imitation parts, technician experience, whether a warranty and tax invoice are included, and hidden fees like night surcharges. Always ask for the price in writing before work begins.
Should I repair the compressor or replace the whole AC?+
If the repair costs more than 50 to 60 percent of a new unit of the same capacity, or the unit is over eight years old, replacement usually makes more sense. The technician compares both options with you on site.
Is there an extra fee for night emergency service?+
Not with us. The price is the same at 3 AM as at 3 PM. Some companies add 50 to 100 SAR at night, so ask before you confirm any booking. See our pricing policy.
Does the price change with AC size?+
Yes. A 2-ton unit needs more gas and larger parts than a 1-ton, so the cost rises with capacity. Central systems are always priced after an on-site inspection.
Is a very low price over the phone a warning sign?+
Often. A final price given without seeing the unit usually grows once the technician arrives. A fair company gives a range by phone and a fixed price in writing after inspection.

How Customers Find Us

How people search for prices, and what the numbers depend on.

Price searches are the most direct ones. Nobody types them idly; they have a quote in one hand or a call still to make. Here's how the questions come in, and what the real answer is.

Gas refill prices

Gas is the most searched line item. People ask “ac gas refill cost”, “how much does it cost to refill ac gas” and “home ac gas refill cost”. They get specific about size, with “1 ton ac gas refilling price” and “gas refilling cost in 1.5 ton ac”, or about the type of unit, such as “window ac gas filling charges 1.5 ton”. Others look at the gas itself: “ac gas price”, “ac 410 gas price” and “r22 refrigerant price”. And a few search for the job by name, “ac gas filling charges”, “ac gas charging” or “ac gas near me”.

Parts and bigger jobs

Compressor questions come up as “ac compressor price” and “compressor 1.5 ton price”. Coil work appears as “air conditioner coil replacement cost”. These are the jobs where a written quote after inspection matters most, because the range is wide.

Cleaning and servicing prices

For the routine end, we see “ac cleaning cost near me” and “split ac service charges”. Both start with what is included, so check that a quote covers the outdoor unit and the drain, not just the filter.

Price-comparison wording

Comparison shoppers search “ac price” on its own, and others search “ac compressor repair near me” once they already suspect that's the fault, usually after a diagnostic visit elsewhere.
