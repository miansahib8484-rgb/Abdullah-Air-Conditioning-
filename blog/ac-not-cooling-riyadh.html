7 Reasons Your AC Is Not Cooling — 7 أسباب لعدم تبريد المكيف

1

Dirty Air Filter

The most common cause of weak cooling in Riyadh is a filter clogged with dust. In Riyadh's dusty environment, filters fill up faster than in almost any other city. A clogged filter blocks airflow, cuts cooling efficiency, and raises your electricity bill.

Do it yourself: open the indoor unit cover, take out the filter and wash it with water or vacuum it. Let it dry completely before refitting. If cooling doesn't improve, the problem is elsewhere.
2

Low Refrigerant (Gas Refill Needed)

Low refrigerant is the second most common cause of weak cooling. Gas doesn't naturally get consumed, a shortage means there's a leak in the cooling circuit that must be fixed before recharging with new gas.

Signs of low gas:
  • Weak cooling despite running for hours
  • Frost on the copper pipes behind the indoor unit
  • Frost or ice forming on the indoor coil
  • A light hissing sound
  • The outdoor unit runs continuously without stopping
Never recharge gas without checking for a leak first! Adding gas when there's an active leak wastes money, it will escape again within weeks. Our technicians check for leaks with specialized equipment and fix them before charging the precise gas amount.
3

Dirty Condenser Coils

In Riyadh, dust builds up on the outdoor coil fins extremely fast. A clogged coil can't release heat efficiently, which raises pressure in the cooling circuit and reduces cooling capacity. The fix: clean the outdoor unit at least every 6 months, especially after sandstorms.

4

Outdoor Fan Not Working

If the outdoor unit is running but its fan isn't spinning, heat can't be released and the compressor protects itself and shuts down. You can check visually from outside, if you see the compressor running with the fan stopped, call us immediately, because the compressor is at risk.

5

Failing Compressor

The compressor is the heart of the AC. If it's starting to fail, you'll notice: very weak cooling, a booming or knocking sound during operation, or the unit shutting down repeatedly after a few minutes. Repairing the compressor early is much cheaper than replacing it, don't ignore the signs.

6

Thermostat / PCB Fault

Sometimes the AC seems to run normally, but the thermostat isn't sending the correct demand signal to the compressor. Result: the fan runs but the compressor doesn't start, and the air coming out is warm, not cold. This needs a technician to check the board and either reset or replace it.

7

Undersized AC Unit

If you're using a 12,000-BTU unit in a room that needs 18,000, the AC will run continuously without reaching the target temperature. This isn't a fault, it's a sizing mistake. Our technicians calculate the right capacity for each room and recommend the best fix.

Cases You Can Diagnose Yourself First

Noticing AC freezing up and not cooling? Turn off the AC for two hours to let the ice melt, then turn it back on. If the problem continues, that confirms one of the seven causes above. Weak air flow from AC without freezing is usually a dirty filter that can be cleaned in 10 minutes. And if you hear AC making noise repair alongside weak cooling, that calls for an immediate compressor check before the fault worsens and repair costs rise.

Quick Diagnostic Steps Before Calling a Technician

Before booking a visit, try these simple steps: make sure the thermostat isn't the issue by setting the temperature to the lowest setting and confirming the AC responds. Check for obvious signs of a refrigerant leak, like oily spots around the copper connections. If it's simply a dirty filter, clean it first, which fixes more cases than you'd expect, at no cost.

If the compressor isn't working, the outdoor unit is dead or a fan isn't spinning alongside the cooling problem, mention it when you call. It helps our technician bring the right parts on the first visit instead of a second trip.

Brand-Specific "Not Cooling" Cases We See Often

Owners of LG and Gree units, window units and split units describe the same handful of problems in different words: the AC works but doesn't cool, the fan runs while the air stays warm, or it stopped cooling suddenly. Even after a cleaning, if the refrigerant wasn't checked, cooling can still be weak. If your unit also has a humming compressor, vibration you can feel through the wall, or switches itself off after a few minutes, these usually point to the compressor or the electrical protection circuit, not the refrigerant.

Special Cases: Split AC Leaking or Sudden Shutdown

If your unit is a split rather than a central system, check the outdoor unit first, because most split faults originate outdoors, not indoors. If you're noticing water leaking alongside weak cooling, that usually confirms a blocked indoor drain path rather than low gas. A split AC that suddenly stops cooling after years of normal operation calls for an immediate compressor check before the fault worsens. And if it isn't cold even after a recent cleaning, that points to a gas or electrical issue, not cleanliness.

FAQ — الأسئلة الشائعة

FAQ About an AC That Is Not Cooling

Why is my AC running but not cooling?+
The most common causes are a dirty filter or coil, low refrigerant, a failed capacitor, a blocked outdoor unit and a compressor fault. Checking the filter and outdoor unit takes two minutes; gas and compressor issues need a technician.
Is it safe to keep running an AC that isn't cooling?+
Not for long. A unit with low gas or a weak capacitor makes the compressor work harder, which can turn a 150 SAR repair into a 900 SAR one. Switch it off and get it checked.
Why is ice forming on my AC?+
Ice usually means restricted airflow, such as a clogged filter or dirty coil, or low refrigerant. Turn the AC off, run only the fan for an hour to melt it, and clean the filter. If ice returns, it needs a gas and leak check.
Why is water leaking from my AC?+
Usually a blocked drain line or a frozen coil melting. Dust and algae clog the drain in Riyadh, and a professional flush normally solves it. Persistent dripping after that points to a poorly levelled indoor unit.
Does AC gas run out over time?+
No. Refrigerant circulates in a sealed system, so if it's low there's a leak. Topping up without repairing the leak only buys a few weeks. That's why we test for leaks before every refill.
How do I know if I need a gas refill?+
Signs include weak cooling despite clean filters, ice on the pipe, a hissing sound, oily marks near joints and long running cycles. A technician confirms it with pressure gauges and finds the leak before recharging.
Why does my split AC blow warm air after cleaning?+
Check that the mode is set to cool rather than fan or dry, and that the outdoor unit is actually running. If the outdoor unit runs but air is still warm, a sensor plug or drain connection may have been disturbed, or a small leak was hidden by dirt. Call for a re-check.
Why does my window AC run but not cool enough?+
Window units lose performance quickly when the coil is caked with dust, the seal around the frame leaks hot air, or the unit is too small for the room. Cleaning and resealing solve the first two.
How much does it cost to fix an AC that isn't cooling?+
A filter or drain fix starts from 150 SAR, a gas refill is 150 to 300 SAR, and a compressor job starts from 900 SAR. We diagnose first and quote in writing, so you decide before we start.
When should I replace an old AC instead of repairing it?+
When it's over eight to ten years old and the repair costs more than half of a new unit, especially if it uses old R22 gas that's now expensive to find.

How Customers Find Us — كيف يجدنا العملاء

How Customers Find Us

The many ways people describe an AC that runs but does not cool.

"Not cooling" covers a dozen different faults, and the wording people use often hints at which one it's.

The general complaint

The core searches are “ac not cooling”, “air conditioner is not cooling”, “aircon not cooling” and “why my ac not cooling”. Slightly more specific are “ac not blowing cold air” and “air conditioner not blowing cold air but running”, which tell us the fan works and the cooling part doesn't.

Window and split units

Owners of older units search “window ac not cooling”, “window ac not cooling but fan is running”, “window ac not cooling enough” and “window ac running but not cooling”. Split-unit owners name their brand: “lg ac working but not cooling”, “lg split ac not cooling” or “gree ac not cooling”. A frustrating one is “split type aircon not cooling after cleaning”, which is usually a settings or sensor issue rather than a bad clean.

Water, ice and compressor symptoms

Symptoms appear as “ac leaking water”, “gree ac leaking water”, “gree ac ice problem” and “a c frozen”. Worse ones are “ac compressor not working”, “compressor not working ac” and “samsung inverter ac compressor not working”. If the compressor is involved, don't keep restarting the unit.

When gas is the suspect

Many readers jump to gas and search “ac gas refill”, “ac gas filling”, “ac gas filling near me” and “ac gas refilling near me”. Others ask about the gas itself with “air conditioner freon” or “how to put freon on split type aircon”. The short answer is that gas is sealed in the system, so if it's low there's a leak, and that has to be found before recharging.

Leak-specific searches

Leak searches get their own wording: “aircon leaking water”, “air conditioner leaking water”, “ac unit leaking water” and just “leaking air conditioner” or “ac leak”. Most of the time it's “water dripping from ac indoor unit” rather than the outdoor unit, which usually means a blocked drain rather than a refrigerant problem.
