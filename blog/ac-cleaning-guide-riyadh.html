DIY Filter Cleaning Steps — تنظيف الفلتر بنفسك

1

Turn Off & Unplug

Turn off the AC from the remote, then switch off the power at the distribution panel. Wait 10-15 minutes for the coil to fully cool.

2

Remove Filters

Gently lift the front cover of the indoor unit. You'll find the filters (usually two pieces) — pull them out carefully. Watch for any wires connected to the cover.

3

Clean the Filters

Gently shake off loose dust. Then wash under cold water with a soft brush. Don't use hot water or harsh soap. Let them air-dry completely — don't reinstall while wet.

4

Clean Inside Unit

Wipe interior surfaces with a damp cloth. You can gently blow dust off the coil with compressed air. Never spray water directly onto the coil or electronic parts.

5

Reassemble & Test

Make sure the filters are 100% dry, put them back gently, close the cover, and turn on the AC. You should notice improved airflow and better cooling within 15-20 minutes.

FAQ About AC Cleaning

How often should I clean my AC in Riyadh?+
In Riyadh, because of heavy dust, we recommend cleaning your AC twice a year: once in April-May before peak season, and once in September after it. Regular cleaning saves 30% on your electricity bill and extends the AC's lifespan.
How much does split AC cleaning cost in Riyadh?+
Split AC cleaning in Riyadh starts from 120 SAR for basic cleaning. A full chemical wash of both indoor and outdoor units starts from 180 SAR. A 3-AC package starts from 450 SAR. Prices include drain clearing and anti-bacterial treatment.
Can I clean my AC myself, or should I call a technician?+
You can clean the visible filter yourself monthly. But deep chemical cleaning of the coil needs a specialist technician — the coil is very thin and damages easily. Drain clearing and pressure-cleaning the outdoor unit also require specialized equipment.
What's the difference between basic cleaning and a chemical wash?+
Basic cleaning: filters only, washed with water — can be done monthly at home. Full chemical wash: includes cleaning the coil with specialized chemicals to remove algae, bacteria, and deep dirt — recommended twice a year.
