AC Cleaning Service in Riyadh: Chemical Wash, Filter and Drain Guide

Dust is the number one enemy of an air conditioner in Riyadh. This guide explains when to clean your AC, what a full chemical wash includes, how to clean the filter safely yourself, and what a professional cleaning costs.

Quick answer: In Riyadh, rinse split AC filters every two to four weeks in summer and book a professional cleaning twice a year, in April and October. Professional cleaning starts from 120 SAR per unit and reaches the indoor coil, the drain and the outdoor unit, which a filter rinse can't.

Key Benefit: A clean filter and coil let the AC reach the set temperature sooner and switch off, so it uses less electricity. How much you save depends on how dirty the unit was, but most customers notice it on the next bill.

Two problems we hear about most are water dripping from the indoor unit and a musty smell after a long period without cleaning. Usually the cause is the same: a dirty filter or a clogged drain path. Regular cleaning also prevents weak airflow and keeps cooling efficient for as long as possible without needing a technician.

When to Clean Your AC in Riyadh — متى تنظف مكيفك؟

🗓️ Monthly
Filter Cleaning

Remove the filters and clean them with a vacuum and water. A quick 10-minute task that heads off many weak-cooling problems.

📅 Every 6 Months
Chemical Wash ⭐

Best timing: April (before summer) and October (after it). Includes cleaning the coil, drain, and outdoor unit.

🌪️ After Sandstorms
Emergency Cleaning

After every sandstorm, clean the outdoor unit and indoor filters, heavy dust can clog the unit within hours.

DIY Filter Cleaning Steps — تنظيف الفلتر بنفسك

1

Turn Off & Unplug

Turn off the AC from the remote, then switch off the power at the distribution panel. Wait 10-15 minutes for the coil to fully cool.

2

Remove Filters

Gently lift the front cover of the indoor unit. You'll find the filters (usually two pieces), pull them out carefully. Watch for any wires connected to the cover.

3

Clean the Filters

Gently shake off loose dust. Then wash under cold water with a soft brush. Don't use hot water or harsh soap. Let them air-dry completely, don't reinstall while wet.

4

Clean Inside Unit

Wipe interior surfaces with a damp cloth. You can gently blow dust off the coil with compressed air. Never spray water directly onto the coil or electronic parts.

5

Reassemble & Test

Make sure the filters are 100% dry, put them back gently, close the cover, and turn on the AC. You should notice improved airflow and better cooling within 15-20 minutes.

DIY Filter Cleaning Guide — تنظيف الفلتر بنفسك

You can clean a dirty filter yourself in about 10 minutes: turn off the AC, open the indoor unit cover, remove the filter and wash it with lukewarm water and mild soap, then dry it completely before reinstalling. That stops weak cooling caused by simple blockage. If the problem continues after cleaning, the cause is deeper, such as low gas or a failing capacitor, and needs a technician's inspection.

FAQ About AC Cleaning in Riyadh

How often should I clean my AC in Riyadh?+
Twice a year: April before peak heat, and October after it. If you live in a dusty area, near construction or keep pets, every three to four months is safer.
How much does an AC cleaning service cost in Riyadh?+
From 120 SAR per unit. Homes with several units usually get a discount when they're cleaned in the same visit.
What is included in a chemical wash?+
Indoor coil and blower wash, filter cleaning, drain-line flush, outdoor condenser rinse, an anti-bacterial treatment and a performance test at the end. We protect floors and furniture during the job.
How long does an AC cleaning take?+
About an hour per split unit, longer if the drain is badly blocked or the outdoor unit is hard to reach.
Will cleaning lower my electricity bill?+
Usually, yes. A clogged coil forces the compressor to run longer, so a clean unit reaches the set temperature sooner. How much you save depends on how dirty the unit was, but most customers notice a difference within a month.
Can I clean the filter myself?+
Yes, and you should, every two to four weeks in summer. Switch the unit off, slide out the filter, rinse with lukewarm water, dry it fully and refit it. Don't spray water inside the unit or use a pressure washer on the coil.
Why does my AC smell musty after a few months?+
Moisture on the coil and drain pan grows mould and bacteria, which the fan then blows into the room. A chemical wash and drain flush removes it at the source.
Should I clean before summer or after?+
Both, if you can. Before summer restores full cooling capacity; after summer removes the season's dust and moisture before the unit sits idle over winter.
