When It's a True AC Emergency — متى تحتاج خدمة طوارئ فعلاً

🔥 Complete Shutdown in Extreme Heat

If your AC stops completely with outdoor temperatures above 40°C, especially with children or elderly people at home, this counts as AC broken fix immediately — don't wait until morning.

⚡ Burning Smell or Electrical Spark

Turn off the AC at the breaker immediately and call us. This is a genuine warning sign that needs urgent AC maintenance service before a fire or short circuit occurs.

💧 Heavy Water Leak Inside the Home

A minor drip can wait for a regular appointment, but a heavy leak threatening floors and furniture needs fast AC failure emergency repair.

🏢 Commercial Property Breakdown

A restaurant, clinic, or office without AC means direct financial loss. We offer commercial AC repair riyadh with emergency priority for business owners.

Cases That Aren't Real Emergencies

Not every fault is an emergency. If your AC is running but cooling slightly weaker than usual, or you hear a light, non-disruptive sound, these are usually regular professional AC maintenance service cases that can be scheduled the next day at a lower price than an emergency visit. Call us and describe the situation, and we'll tell you honestly whether it needs an immediate visit or not.

What You Can Do Yourself Before the Technician Arrives

  • Check the circuit breaker — sometimes it's just a tripped breaker, not an actual AC fault.
  • Check the remote and its batteries — a common simple cause that prevents any response from the unit.
  • If there's a water leak, place a towel or container under the indoor unit to protect the floor and furniture.
  • Don't try to open the outdoor unit or tamper with the refrigerant yourself — this needs a certified technician with specialized equipment.
  • If you smell burning, turn off the unit at the main breaker immediately and call us directly.

FAQ About Emergency Service

Do you really arrive within 30 minutes?+
Yes, this is our typical time in most Riyadh districts thanks to geographically distributed technicians. In cases of severe traffic or very distant areas, it may reach 40-45 minutes, and we'll tell you the estimated time as soon as you call.
Is there an extra fee for nighttime emergency service?+
No. Same price day and night, on weekends and holidays. This is a deliberate choice — Riyadh's heat doesn't discriminate by time, and we believe emergency service shouldn't be exploited.
What should I do while waiting for the technician?+
Turn off the AC at the breaker if you hear a strange sound or smell burning. Close curtains to reduce indoor heat. Don't try to open the outdoor unit yourself. If there's a water leak, place a towel or container under the indoor unit.
What counts as an AC emergency?+
Complete cooling failure during extreme heat (above 40°C), burning smells or sparks from the unit, water leaking heavily into the home, or unusual loud noises accompanied by loss of cooling. Any of these warrant an emergency call rather than waiting for a scheduled appointment.
How much does the diagnostic fee cost on an emergency visit?+
The diagnostic visit ranges from 100-150 SAR, fully deducted if you agree to have us complete the repair. No hidden fees or invoice surprises.
