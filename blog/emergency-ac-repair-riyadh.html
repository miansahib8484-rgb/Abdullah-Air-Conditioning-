When It's a True AC Emergency — متى تحتاج خدمة طوارئ فعلاً

🔥 Complete Shutdown in Extreme Heat

If your AC stops completely with outdoor temperatures above 40°C, especially with children or elderly people at home, this counts as AC broken fix immediately, don't wait until morning.

⚡ Burning Smell or Electrical Spark

Turn off the AC at the breaker immediately and call us. This is a genuine warning sign that needs urgent attention before a fire or short circuit occurs.

💧 Heavy Water Leak Inside the Home

A minor drip can wait for a regular appointment, but a heavy leak threatening floors and furniture needs fast AC failure emergency repair.

🏢 Commercial Property Breakdown

A restaurant, clinic, or office without AC means direct financial loss. We give commercial customers emergency priority.

Cases That Aren't Real Emergencies

Not every fault is an emergency. If your AC is running but cooling slightly weaker than usual, or you hear a light, non-disruptive sound, these are usually regular maintenance cases that can be scheduled the next day at a lower price than an emergency visit. Call us and describe the situation, and we'll tell you honestly whether it needs an immediate visit or not.

What You Can Do Yourself Before the Technician Arrives

  • Check the circuit breaker, sometimes it's just a tripped breaker, not an actual AC fault.
  • Check the remote and its batteries, a common simple cause that prevents any response from the unit.
  • If there's a water leak, place a towel or container under the indoor unit to protect the floor and furniture.
  • Don't try to open the outdoor unit or tamper with the refrigerant yourself, this needs a certified technician with specialized equipment.
  • If you smell burning, turn off the unit at the main breaker immediately and call us directly.

FAQ About Emergency AC Repair

How fast can an emergency AC technician reach me in Riyadh?+
In most districts, about 30 minutes, day or night. When you call we give you a real arrival time. If traffic will make it longer, we say so at once instead of promising and turning up late.
Is there an extra charge for night, weekend or holiday calls?+
No. The price is identical at any hour, on Fridays and on public holidays. The only cost is the repair itself, quoted before we start.
What counts as an AC emergency?+
A complete shutdown in extreme heat, a burning smell, smoke or sparks, water leaking near electrical parts, or anyone at home who is elderly, very young or unwell. If the unit is merely weak, a same-day booking is usually enough.
What should I do while waiting for the technician?+
Switch the AC off, open curtains to a shaded room, run fans and keep doors closed to hold the cool air. If there was a burning smell or the breaker tripped, leave the unit off at the breaker and don't keep restarting it.
My AC smells like burning. Is that dangerous?+
It can be. A burning smell often means an overheating motor, a failing capacitor or a wiring fault. Turn it off at the breaker and call us; don't run it to see if the smell goes away.
Will the technician fix it during the first visit?+
Usually. Our vans carry capacitors, fan motors, sensors and gas. If a specific part is needed, we order the original, tell you the timing and make the unit as safe as we can in the meantime.
Why does my AC trip the breaker every time I switch it on?+
Typically a shorted compressor, a failed capacitor or damaged wiring. Don't keep resetting the breaker. Leave the unit off and call so we can test it safely.
Do you handle emergencies for washing machines and fridges as well?+
Yes. A fridge that stops cooling is urgent because food spoils, so we treat it the same way as an AC breakdown and send someone as soon as we can.

How Customers Find Us

What people type in the first few minutes of a breakdown.

Emergency searches are short and typed fast, usually from the very room that has gone hot. They tend to fall into a few patterns.

Describing what just happened

The commonest are “split ac suddenly stopped cooling”, “our aircon is not cooling” and “split system blowing hot air”. Others notice the moving parts: “ac fan is not spinning” or “ac fan not working”, and “ac compressor making humming noise”. A frightened few ask “why ac compressor is not turning on” or “ac compressor is not engaging”. If any of that's you and there's a burning smell, switch the unit off at the breaker and call.

Asking for a fast service

When people stop diagnosing and start asking for help, the wording becomes “aircon repair service”, “air conditioning repair service” and “hvac repair service”, or “ac fixing service”, “repair ac service”, “ac repair servicing” and even “hvac support”. The words differ; the need doesn't: somebody has to come today.

Same idea, different words

The same urgency shows up as “emergency air conditioning service”, plain “emergency air conditioning”, or simply “aircon ac repair” typed in a hurry with no other words attached.
