6 Common Fridge Faults in Riyadh

1. Fridge Not Cooling Despite Running

The most common cause of refrigerator repair riyadh calls. Usually needs refrigerator compressor repair, a refrigerant leak fix, or a faulty thermostat not sending the correct cooling signal.

2. Freezer Not Freezing Properly

A common freezer not freezing repair issue caused by heavy ice buildup blocking cold airflow, or a fault in the automatic defrost system.

3. Water Leaking Under the Fridge

The most common refrigerator water leaking fix case is caused by a blocked internal drain path. Simple cleaning solves it within minutes in most cases.

4. Strange Noise or Constant Knocking

fridge making noise repair — usually a fan touching built-up ice, or a compressor bearing starting to wear with prolonged use.

5. Door Seal Not Closing Tightly

Needs a fridge door seal replacement when the rubber hardens or cracks. This noticeably raises electricity use because the compressor runs longer to compensate for cold air escaping.

6. Thermostat Not Responding

refrigerator thermostat repair is needed when the internal temperature becomes inconsistent — too cold in one spot and warm in another within the same fridge.

If you searched fridge condenser coils, here's why it matters: dust builds up on the coils behind or underneath your refrigerator over time, especially in Riyadh's dusty climate. Blocked coils make the compressor work harder, raise your electricity bill, and eventually cause weak cooling that looks like a bigger problem than it is. FAQ About Fridge Repair

Why is my fridge not cooling despite running?+
Common causes: a weak or failing compressor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty thermostat, or the internal cooling fan stopped. First step: make sure the fridge isn't overpacked in a way that blocks cold air circulation. If the problem continues, a technical inspection is needed to pinpoint the exact cause.
My fridge is leaking water on the floor — why?+
Usually the internal drain path is blocked with ice or dirt, causing water to overflow instead of collecting in the evaporation tray under the fridge. The fix is usually clearing the drain path, taking 20-30 minutes from a specialist technician.
How much does compressor repair cost in Riyadh?+
Fridge compressor replacement ranges from 400-900 SAR depending on fridge size and brand, including the part, labor, and warranty. If the fridge is over 10 years old, we recommend comparing the repair cost to a new fridge's price before proceeding.
Can a refrigerator be repaired instead of replaced?+
In most cases yes — compressor failures, thermostat issues, and door seal problems are all repairable. Replacement is usually the smarter choice only when the unit is 10+ years old and the repair cost exceeds 40-50% of a new refrigerator's price.
Do you repair all fridge brands?+
Yes — Samsung, LG, Gree, Midea, Haier, White Westinghouse, and every brand available in the Saudi market. Our technicians are trained on different cooling systems and carry common spare parts with them.
