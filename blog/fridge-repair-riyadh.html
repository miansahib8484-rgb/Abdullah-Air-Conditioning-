6 Common Fridge Faults in Riyadh

1. Fridge Not Cooling Despite Running

The most common cause of fridge repair calls. Usually needs a compressor repair, a refrigerant leak fix, or a faulty thermostat not sending the correct cooling signal.

2. Freezer Not Freezing Properly

A common freezer not freezing repair issue caused by heavy ice buildup blocking cold airflow, or a fault in the automatic defrost system.

3. Water Leaking Under the Fridge

The most common leak is caused by a blocked internal drain path. Simple cleaning solves it within minutes in most cases.

4. Strange Noise or Constant Knocking

Usually a fan touching built-up ice, or a compressor bearing starting to wear with prolonged use.

5. Door Seal Not Closing Tightly

Needs a new door seal when the rubber hardens or cracks. This noticeably raises electricity use because the compressor runs longer to compensate for cold air escaping.

6. Thermostat Not Responding

A thermostat repair is needed when the internal temperature becomes inconsistent, too cold in one spot and warm in another within the same fridge.

Dirty Fridge Condenser Coils — A Cause People Often Miss

If you have never cleaned the coils behind your fridge, here's why it matters: dust builds up on the coils behind or underneath your refrigerator over time, especially in Riyadh's dusty climate. Blocked coils make the compressor work harder, raise your electricity bill, and eventually cause weak cooling that looks like a bigger problem than it's. A technician visit that includes a coil clean is often the cheapest fix on this whole page, sometimes under 100 SAR.

Repair or Replace? When to Buy a New Fridge

General rule: if the fridge is under 8 years old, repair is usually the smarter economic choice even if a compressor repair is needed. But if it's over 10-12 years old and the repair cost approaches 40-50% of a new fridge's price, replacement becomes more sensible long-term, especially given the efficiency gap between older and newer models.

FAQ About Fridge Repair in Riyadh

Why is my fridge not cooling even though the light is on?+
The light only proves the fridge has power. Common causes are a failed start relay or compressor, dusty condenser coils, a stopped evaporator fan, a faulty thermostat or a worn door seal. A technician tests each in turn.
How much does fridge repair cost in Riyadh?+
From 150 SAR including diagnosis. A compressor replacement usually costs 400 to 900 SAR depending on size and brand, including part, labour and warranty.
Is it worth repairing a 10-year-old fridge?+
If the repair costs more than 40 to 50 percent of a new fridge of the same size, replacement is usually smarter. For a compressor problem on a well-kept 8-year-old unit, repair often wins. We'll tell you which side of the line yours is on.
Why is water leaking under my fridge?+
Most often the defrost drain is blocked with ice or debris, so meltwater overflows onto the floor. Clearing the drain takes about half an hour. A cracked water tube on a dispenser model is another cause.
Why does my fridge make a loud noise?+
A quiet hum is normal. Rattling or grinding usually means a fan blade rubbing on ice or a worn compressor mount. Turn it off and call if the noise is loud or continuous.
My fridge stopped working. How long does food stay safe?+
Roughly four hours in the fridge and up to a day or two in a full freezer if you keep the doors closed. Call us straight away; we treat a dead fridge as urgent.
Which fridge brands do you repair?+
Samsung, LG, Haier, Midea, Gree, White Westinghouse and most other brands sold in Saudi Arabia, in single-door, double-door and side-by-side models.
Do you refill fridge gas?+
Yes. As with air conditioners, gas doesn't run out by itself, so we find and repair the leak first and then recharge to the correct specification.
