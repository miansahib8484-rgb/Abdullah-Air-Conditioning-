The 6 Most Common Washing Machine Faults — أعطال الغسالات الشائعة

1

Washing Machine Not Spinning

The drum fills and washes but won't spin, so clothes come out soaking. Causes range from an unbalanced or overloaded drum to a worn drive belt or motor coupling, a door lock that won't engage, or a control board fault.

Try first: Pause the cycle, redistribute the load and remove heavy items such as towels or blankets, then run a spin-only cycle.
2

Washing Machine Not Draining

Water stays in the drum at the end of the cycle. The usual suspects are a clogged drain filter, a kinked or blocked hose, or a failed drain pump that hums but doesn't move water.

Try first: Unplug the machine, place a shallow tray and towel, and clean the drain filter behind the small panel at the bottom front.
3

Leaking Water

A puddle under the machine usually comes from a worn door seal on front-loaders, a loose inlet or drain hose, a cracked pump housing, or too much detergent overflowing from the drawer.

Try first: Turn off the tap, unplug the machine and check the hose connections and the rubber door seal for cracks or trapped debris.
4

Won't Start or the Door Will Not Lock

If the display lights up but nothing happens, the door latch or lid switch is often the culprit. A machine that dies mid-cycle can also point to a thermal fuse, the control board or a power supply issue.

Try first: Close the door firmly, check nothing is caught in the seal, and try another wall socket.
5

Banging, Grinding or a Very Loud Spin

A machine that walks across the floor is usually unlevelled or overloaded. A deep grinding or rumbling that gets worse at high speed points to a worn drum bearing, which is one of the bigger repairs.

Try first: Check the machine is level on all four feet and that transit bolts were removed on new machines.
6

Musty Smell or Black Mould

Warm, damp detergent residue builds up in the door seal and drawer, especially on front-loaders in a closed laundry room. It isn't a mechanical fault, but it's the most common complaint we clean up.

Try first: Run a hot empty cycle with washing-machine cleaner, wipe the seal and leave the door ajar between loads.

Washer Technician or New Machine: Repair or Replace?

A simple rule works well. If the repair costs more than about half the price of a comparable new machine, replacement usually makes sense. If the machine is under eight years old and the fault is a pump, lock, hose, sensor or control board, repair is nearly always better value. A worn drum bearing plus a weak motor on an older washer is the classic case where we'll tell you replacing is the smarter move. We give you the repair figure in writing so you can compare it with a new machine before deciding.

Five Habits That Keep a Washer Running

  • Don't overload the drum, and balance heavy items such as blankets and towels.
  • Use the recommended amount of detergent. Extra suds cause leaks and residue.
  • Clean the drain filter and the detergent drawer every month.
  • Wipe the door seal and leave the door open between washes to prevent mould.
  • Keep the machine level and the inlet hoses free of kinks, especially after moving it.

FAQ About Washing Machine Repair in Riyadh

Why is my washing machine not spinning?+
Common causes are an unbalanced or overloaded drum, a worn drive belt or motor coupling, a door lock that won't engage, or a control board fault. Redistribute the load and try a spin cycle; if it still won't spin, call us.
Why is my washing machine not draining?+
Usually a clogged drain filter, a kinked or blocked hose, or a failed drain pump. Cleaning the filter is a safe first check; a humming pump that moves no water needs replacing.
How much does washing machine repair cost in Riyadh?+
From 130 SAR depending on the fault. Simple fixes such as a filter, hose or door latch cost less than a drain pump, motor or control board. We give the exact figure in writing after diagnosis.
Do you repair both front-load and top-load washers?+
Yes, plus semi-automatic and fully automatic models, from Samsung, LG, Haier, Toshiba, White Westinghouse, Kiriazi and other brands.
Can you repair my washing machine at home?+
Yes, most repairs are done at your home in one visit. A badly worn drum bearing can take longer, and we tell you at the visit if that's the case.
Why is water leaking from the bottom of my washer?+
The usual sources are a worn door seal, a loose inlet or drain hose, a cracked pump housing or an overfull detergent drawer. Turn off the water and unplug the washer before calling.
Is it worth repairing an old washing machine?+
If the repair costs more than about half of a comparable new machine, or the drum bearing and motor are both worn, replacement usually makes more sense. For pumps, locks and sensors on a machine under eight years old, repair is nearly always better value.
How often should I clean my washing machine?+
Run a hot empty cycle with a washing-machine cleaner about once a month, wipe the door seal and leave the door ajar between loads. It prevents odours and drain clogs.

Does the repair come with a warranty?+
