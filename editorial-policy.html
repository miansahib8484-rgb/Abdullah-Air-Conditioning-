Why this page exists: advice about AC gas, electrics and appliances can cost you money or safety if it is wrong. Here is how we try to make sure ours isn't.

How We Write and Review Our Guides

1. Who writes the guides

Every guide on this site is published by Abdullah Air Conditioning, a Saudi company repairing air conditioners, washing machines and refrigerators in Riyadh since 2009. The guides are written by our technical team and cover the faults we deal with in Riyadh homes and offices: dust, summer heat, voltage swings and older units that have run for years.

2. What the advice is based on

  • Our own repair work and the questions customers ask us.
  • Manufacturer manuals and service documentation for error codes and specifications. Codes differ between brands and even between model ranges, so we say when a table is a guide and not a diagnosis.
  • Our own current prices. The figures in the guides are our starting prices, as listed on our warranty and pricing page. They are not market averages.

3. How guides are checked

Before a guide is published or materially changed, one of our senior technicians checks the technical steps and the prices in it. Where a named reviewer is shown on a guide, that person has checked it. Each guide shows the date it was last updated.

4. How often we update

We review prices and technical guidance at least twice a year, and sooner if our prices, warranty terms or the products on the market change.

5. Safety

We only recommend do-it-yourself steps that are safe for a non-specialist: cleaning a filter, checking a breaker, looking for a blocked drain. Anything involving refrigerant gas, capacitors, control boards or live wiring is for a technician, and the guides say so.

6. Corrections

If you spot something wrong or out of date, tell us on WhatsApp or call +966 55 292 5939. We correct errors as soon as we can verify them.
