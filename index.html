FAQ — الأسئلة الشائعة

Questions Riyadh Customers Ask Us Most

Straight answers on prices, arrival times, warranty and coverage.

Is there an AC repair technician near me in Riyadh right now?+
Yes. Our technicians are spread across the city, so most districts, from Al Malqa and Hittin in the north to Ash Shifa in the south, get a visit in about 30 minutes. Call or WhatsApp +966 55 292 5939 with your district and we confirm a time within minutes.
How much does AC repair cost in Riyadh?+
Repairs start from 150 SAR, including the visit and diagnosis. Chemical cleaning starts from 120 SAR and a gas refill from 150 SAR. We give you the final price in writing before any work begins, so the invoice matches the quote. See the full price guide.
How fast can you come in an emergency?+
In most districts, about 30 minutes, at any hour including holidays. There's no extra night, weekend or holiday charge. If traffic is heavy around Olaya or King Fahd Road we give you a realistic arrival time when we confirm the booking.
Which AC brands do you repair?+
Gree, LG, Samsung, Midea, Daikin, Carrier, Haier, Mitsubishi, Trane, Voltas, O General, York, TCL, Blue Star and most other brands sold in Saudi Arabia. Vans carry common parts for popular models; for rarer units we order the original part and tell you the timing up front.
My AC is running but the room stays hot. What should I check first?+
Check that the filter is clean, the outdoor unit isn't blocked by dust or debris, and the set temperature is lower than the room. If air is still weak or warm, the cause is usually low refrigerant or a weak capacitor. Switch it off and call, because running it can damage the compressor. More in our not-cooling guide.
What warranty do you give on AC repairs?+
A written 90-day warranty on the repaired fault and a 1-year written warranty on installations. Both are printed on your tax invoice, and we use genuine manufacturer parts so the warranty stays valid. Full terms are on our warranty and pricing page.
Do you provide an official tax invoice?+
Yes. We're registered with ZATCA and every job comes with an official tax invoice showing the work done, the parts used and the warranty period.
Can you also repair washing machines and refrigerators?+
Yes. Washing machine repair starts from 130 SAR and fridge repair from 150 SAR. If your AC and your washer both need attention, the same technician can often deal with both in one visit. See our washer guide and fridge guide.
How often should an AC be serviced in Riyadh?+
Twice a year: a deep clean in April before the peak heat, and another in October once it eases. In very dusty areas, or homes with pets, every three to four months is safer. Regular cleaning keeps airflow strong and helps the compressor last.
Do you work on villas, offices and shops, or only apartments?+
All of them. We repair split, window and central systems in apartments, villas, compounds, offices and shops. Property managers can ask about annual maintenance contracts, which start from 800 SAR per year.

How Customers Find Us — كيف يجدنا العملاء

How Customers Find Us

The searches that bring people to us, and what happens after the call.

Hardly anyone types our name into Google when the AC dies at night. They grab the phone, type whatever comes to mind and call the first number that answers. Here's what those searches look like from our side of the phone.

The "right now" searches

The phrases we see most are “ac repair near me”, “aircon repair near me” and “air conditioning repair near me”, usually typed with one thumb from a hot living room. Some people want an instant answer and go for “fix ac near me” or “repair air conditioner near me”. Others want a person, so they search “ac repairman near me” and expect a name and a time. Whichever wording you use, the answer is the same: give us your district and we tell you when a technician can be there.

Describing the problem

A second group starts with the fault rather than the place. They search “air conditioner repair”, “aircon repair” or “air conditioning repair” and add the neighbourhood later. Some are asking how to do it themselves: “fix air conditioner”, “fixing an air conditioner”, “repair the air conditioner”. If the fault is a dirty filter or a flat remote battery, you can sort it in minutes, and we'd rather tell you that on the phone than send someone for nothing. Gas leaks, capacitors and control boards are different, and that's where “repair of aircon” and “repair for air conditioner” searches usually end in a call to us.

Short and blunt

At 2 a.m. nobody writes full sentences. We see “ac fix”, “ac repair”, “air conditioning fix” and “air cond repair near me”. They all mean the same thing and we treat them that way: someone is hot, and we need an address.

By unit type and by place

Most Riyadh homes run split systems, so “split ac repair” and “home ac repair” are the everyday ones. People who already know the city add it: “ac repair riyadh”, or “air conditioner repairing near me” when they want the closest team. We cover every district, so a search from Al Malqa gets the same service as one from Ash Shifa.

Beyond the AC

The same call sometimes turns out to be about the washer or the fridge, which is why “appliance repair near me” and “home appliance repair service” also bring people here. Our technicians repair washing machines and refrigerators too, and can often deal with them in the same visit.

More ways people phrase it

A few more patterns show up again and again. Some people search “ac unit repair” or “ac fix near me”, others go straight for “24 7 ac repair” or “emergency ac service” when it genuinely can't wait, and “emergency air conditioner repair” covers the same ground. “Just fix the cooling” becomes “cooling repair”. And because AC is rarely the only thing that breaks in the same month, “ac and fridge repair near me” and “best appliance repair near me” land here too.

  • North-east and east: Al Nuzha, Al Rawdah, Al Quds, Qurtuba

By brand

Customers often search with the brand name: Gree, LG, Samsung, Midea, Daikin, Carrier, Haier, Mitsubishi, Trane, Voltas, O General, York, TCL, Blue Star, Hitachi and Zamil. Whatever is on your unit, tell us the model and we bring the right parts.
