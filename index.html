What's the difference between R22, R32, and R410A gas?+
R22 is the old gas that damages the ozone layer and has become rare. R410A was the most common in modern ACs until recently. R32 is the new, more efficient and eco-friendly gas, now the standard in new AC units. Our technicians are certified to work with all gas types and precise charge amounts.

Why is electricity bill high with AC?+
The most common causes: a dirty filter blocking airflow, low gas charge making the compressor run longer, or a dirty outdoor unit reducing heat exchange. Fixing these through regular maintenance noticeably lowers why is electricity bill high with AC concerns and improves overall energy efficiency.
