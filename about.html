FAQ — الأسئلة الشائعة

Questions About Our Company

Who we're, how we work and how to check us out before you book.

How long has Abdullah Air Conditioning operated in Riyadh?+
Since 2009. We began as a Saudi family company in Riyadh and have grown into a full team of technicians covering the whole city, while keeping the same rule: quote first, work second, invoice honestly.
Are your technicians certified?+
Yes. Our technicians are certified and trained on the major brands, and the company holds a commercial registration and a ZATCA tax registration. If you want to see credentials before the visit, ask when you book.
How can I tell whether an AC repair company in Riyadh is legitimate?+
Look for an official ZATCA tax invoice, a registered commercial entity, a landline or business number rather than only a personal WhatsApp, and a written warranty. Be careful with anyone who quotes a final price over the phone without seeing the unit.
Where is your office?+
Prince Mohammed bin Faisal bin Turki Road, Al-Quds District, Riyadh 13214. Our technicians come to you, so most customers never need to visit, but you're welcome to drop in during working hours. Call first so we know you're coming.
What makes you different from other AC companies?+
We put the price in writing before starting, we don't charge extra for nights, weekends or holidays, and every repair carries a written 90-day warranty. Customers tell us that clarity matters more to them than the lowest number.
What happens if the same problem comes back after a repair?+
Call us. The repaired fault is covered for 90 days in writing, and we return to check it at no charge if it's the same problem. If it turns out to be a different fault, we explain the difference before quoting. The warranty terms are all on one page.
Do you only work in homes?+
No. We serve apartments, villas, compounds, offices, shops and warehouses. Commercial customers can ask for annual maintenance contracts with scheduled visits and emergency priority.
Which brands are your technicians trained on?+
Gree, LG, Samsung, Midea, Daikin, Carrier, Haier, Mitsubishi, Trane, Voltas, O General, TCL, York, Blue Star and Hitachi for air conditioners, plus Samsung, LG, Haier and Toshiba for washing machines and refrigerators.
