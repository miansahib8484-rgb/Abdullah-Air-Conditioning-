About Abdullah Air Conditioning

A SASO-certified, Saudi-owned AC repair company serving Riyadh since 2009 — 35+ certified technicians, 8,500+ homes served, and a written warranty on everything we do.

Why We Started

We started this company because too many Riyadh families were being burned by "here today, gone tomorrow" AC technicians — no invoice, no warranty, no accountability when a repair failed two weeks later. We built Abdullah Air Conditioning around the opposite idea: certified technicians, written warranties, official tax invoices, and pricing you see before we touch anything.

Transparent Pricing, Always

We give you the price before we start. No hidden fees, no surprises. The invoice always matches what was agreed. Whether you're asking about AC repair cost Riyadh or affordable AC repair service, you get a written quote before any work begins.

Warranty That Means Something

A written warranty isn't just words. If a repair fails within the warranty period, we come back and fix it free — no arguments. That's what AC repair with guarantee should actually mean: warranty on AC installation that covers both parts and labor.

Why the Right Technician Matters

Many customers ask us why professional AC installation matters before they decide. The answer is simple: incorrect installation shortens your unit's lifespan and drives up your electricity bill. That's why we always explain why AC warranty is important and the difference between parts and labor warranty versus extended AC warranty plans before any work begins.

Questions Customers Ask Before Booking

We're often asked should I replace or repair AC when a unit is older — our honest answer depends on the unit's age and the specific fault, not on what's more profitable for us.
