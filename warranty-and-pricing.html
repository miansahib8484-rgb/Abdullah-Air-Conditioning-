Warranty & Pricing Policy

الضمان وسياسة الأسعار
In short: you get a price in writing before we start, you pay when the job is done and checked, and every repair comes with a written warranty on the invoice.

Our Warranty and Pricing Policy

1. How we price a job

The technician diagnoses the fault first and tells you the price before touching anything. You decide whether to go ahead. The price we agree is the price on the invoice.

  • A diagnostic visit costs 50 to 100 SAR. If you go ahead with the repair, that fee comes off the final invoice.
  • There is no extra charge for nights, weekends or public holidays. A 3 AM call costs the same as a 3 PM one.
  • We don't ask for full payment before the work is finished. You pay after you have checked the result.
  • Central and commercial systems are always priced after a site visit, never by phone.

3. Payment and invoices

We accept cash, credit cards and mada. Every job comes with an official tax invoice showing the work done, the parts used and the warranty period. We are registered with ZATCA (zatca.gov.sa).

4. Warranty

  • Repairs: a written 90-day warranty on the repaired fault.
  • Installations: a written 1-year warranty.
  • Parts: we fit genuine manufacturer parts, so the warranty stays valid. If a part is discontinued on an older unit, we tell you at the visit rather than fit something that won't last.

The warranty covers the fault we repaired. A different, unrelated fault is a new job: we diagnose it and quote it before starting, the same as any other.

5. If the same problem comes back

Call or message us on WhatsApp and quote your invoice. If it is the same fault and it is still inside the warranty period, we come back and check it at no charge.

6. Arrival times

Our average arrival time in most Riyadh districts is about 30 minutes. It is an average, not a promise: traffic on Olaya, King Fahd Road or during a dust storm can slow us down. When you book we give you a realistic time, and we tell you straight away if it changes.
